## Quality Checklist for Images

Before uploading to your site, ensure:

- ✅ **Correct Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (1200x800px minimum)
- ✅ **Professional Quality**: No watermarks or low-quality graphics
- ✅ **Color Theme Match**: 
  - Image 1: Blue/Cyan tones
  - Image 2: Gold/Amber tones
  - Image 3: Purple/Pink tones
- ✅ **File Size**: Under 500KB (use TinyPNG to compress)
- ✅ **Subject Matter**: Professional, AI-themed, career-focused
- ✅ **Clarity**: Sharp, well-lit, professional
- ✅ **Consistency**: Similar style and tone across all 3 images

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Using very small images (less than 1000px width)
❌ Using copyright-protected images without permission
❌ Mismatched color schemes between images
❌ Images that don't match the "AI theme"
❌ Generic stock photos without any customization
❌ Images with visible watermarks
❌ Poor quality or blurry images
❌ Images that look obviously AI-generated (if that's not desired)
